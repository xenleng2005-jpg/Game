Our Story

A Taste of Home,
Every Single Day

OAK — Original Anak Kampung — is Malaysia's premier Halal Chinese Muslim eatery, bringing the authentic flavours of northern-inspired cuisine to the heart of Kota Damansara.

We believe great food should be accessible to everyone. That's why we craft every dish with the finest fresh ingredients, offering a 5-star dining experience at truly affordable prices — clean, wholesome, and made with heart.

From our signature Nasi Ayam Hainan to the richly spiced Mee Kari and the beloved Penang Prawn Mee, every bowl and plate carries the soul of Malaysian kampung cooking.
