Our Story

Where Fire Meets Flavour

Nestled in the heart of Jalan Alor — Kuala Lumpur's legendary night food street — Restoran Meng Kee Grill Fish has been delivering unforgettable seafood experiences for decades. Our signature charcoal-grilled fish, fresh off the grill, draws food lovers from every corner of the globe.
