Our Story

A Hidden Gem with Michelin-Star Potential

Tucked beneath a wellness centre in Aman Suria, The Gathering Beast emerges as a sweeping exposed-brick sanctuary — towering glass windows framing potted greenery, antique earthenware and calligraphy on the walls whispering centuries of Chinese heritage.

Our talented chefs infuse modern technique into familiar Malaysian Chinese fare, crafting an experience that is brilliantly unexpected — sometimes daring, always delicious. From 24-hour slow-cooked beef tongue to golden crispy kuey teow in triple-fish stock, every dish tells a story.

Signature Dishes

◆

01

Three Kingdom Beef

An offal lover's triumph — tripe and tendon with celeriac and cashews, dressed in aged balsamic vinegar. Three kingdoms, one glorious plate.

02

Beef Tongue Steak

Slow-cooked at 75°C for 24 hours to silken perfection, glazed with Huangjiu rice wine, finished with pickled nangka and sengkuang.

03

Golden Crispy Kuey Teow

Immersed in rich, steamy broth built from three kinds of fish stock — complete with fresh crab meat and sliced snapper. A masterclass in texture.

04

Fermented Pork Balls

Terrifically textured and wildly tasty, served with a creamy century egg sauce that challenges expectations and rewards the adventurous palate.

05

Grilled Argentinian Squid

Premium Argentinian squid kissed by the grill, charred to smoky perfection with a delicate sweetness. The ocean on a plate.

06

36-Hour Brined Chicken

Patience made edible. Brined for 36 hours, the result is impossibly juicy, golden-skinned perfection — our tribute to Chinese roasting tradition.
