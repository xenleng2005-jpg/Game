Our Story

More Than a Café,
It's an Experience

Nestled at I-Residence, Kota Damansara, Kuppi & Sekut Caffe is a sanctuary of flavor and warmth. With exposed brick walls, warm wooden tones, and natural light — we created a space where every visit feels like coming home.

Our menu blends the best of Western and Malaysian cuisine — from juicy smashed burgers and hearty chicken chops to fragrant seafood fried rice and silky pasta. Pair it with our signature craft coffee for the ultimate experience.
